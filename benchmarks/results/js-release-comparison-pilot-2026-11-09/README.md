# Incomplete benchmark attempt

This attempt did not complete its sampling plan. Retained reports and diagnostics are not a complete release comparison and must not be combined with another host's samples. See run.json for the original plan, interruption and confirmed teardown.

The original plan used five repetitions of 1,000 operations per condition. Its first complete SQLite round across four variants and both concurrency levels showed that the full matrix would exceed the three-hour measurement deadline. The operator stopped the feasibility attempt and retained its eight complete reports plus the partial next report. No samples are discarded or incorporated into the replacement comparison. The replacement reduces the count to 500 for every condition before provisioning a fresh runner; all five repetitions, 100 warmups, versions, scenarios, worker counts, concurrency levels and durability controls remain in place.
